Tip: Open This File In Google Chrome

Small and medium batches work fine in this browser. For very large batches (over about 1 GB), Chrome can save straight to your disk: right-click the file, choose Open With → Google Chrome.

Word, Excel Or PowerPoint Files Will Be Added As Text

    How Your Privacy Is Protected
    • Nothing leaves this computer. This page is a single file that runs inside your browser. It's locked with a security rule that blocks every internet connection, so it physically can't upload, sync or share anything.
    • Nothing is installed or stored. Your files are only read, never copied or changed. When you close this tab, everything the tool held in memory is gone.
    • No logs and no tracking. File names and contents aren't recorded anywhere.
    • You choose where the PDF is saved. Keep it on an encrypted computer (FileVault on Mac, BitLocker on Windows). If you save it to a folder that syncs to iCloud, OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox, that service needs a Business Associate Agreement (BAA). Use Password-Protect before you send the PDF anywhere.
