How It WorksShow
  • Three Active Slots. Only three projects get your energy. To add a fourth, finish one or move one to Waiting.
  • The 21-Day Rule. Anything untouched for 21 days slides to The Bench on its own. Touching it (Did It Today) resets the clock.
  • Recommit Or Bury. Recommitting needs a next physical step and a date. Burying needs one line on what you learned. Buried projects can be brought back.
  • Weekly Check-In. One screen: On Track, Behind, Blocked, Needs Decision, Can Ignore. Copy it into Sovereign or any AI.
  • Private. Everything stays in this browser on this device. The app is locked so it cannot connect to the internet. Save Backup File moves your list to another device.
Why It WorksShow

Writing a specific if-then plan (when and where you will act) reliably helps people follow through. Across 94 independent tests, these plans had a medium-to-large effect on reaching goals (d = .65).

The 21-day rule and the three-slot limit are design choices built on that idea: every live project carries one concrete next step, and anything without one gets a decision instead of quiet guilt.
